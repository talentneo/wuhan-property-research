# Wuhan property report template

## 1. Conclusion first

- One-paragraph verdict: fit for self-use / compare only / wait, budget fit, and decisive reason.
- Table: budget fit, price reasonableness, commute, school certainty, product, liquidity, delivery risk.

## 2. Project identity and official filing

Fields:

- Marketing name, filing name, alias
- District,板块, address, developer, agent/sales telephone if public
- Pre-sale permit / current-sale filing status
- Official total units, sold, for sale
- Intended building/unit sale status, mortgage, seizure, restriction
- Filing unit price/total price and whether the sales discount is written into the contract

If official data is missing, state the query date and exact conclusion, e.g. “No public filing record found”; do not infer that a permit exists.

## 3. Price and transaction evidence

- Developer/listing/reference price
- Filing price
- Recent same-project or same-community transactions
- Comparable projects and second-hand benchmark
- For second-hand homes: listing history, platform transactions, tax/loan/evaluation caveats
- If budget supplied: price per layout, discount scenarios, renovation cost, and total affordability

## 4. Product and buildings

Mandatory fields:

- Building count, product type (洋房/小高层/高层), floors, units per floor/layer
- Floor height if disclosed; otherwise mark unavailable
- Plot ratio, green ratio, planning households, parking ratio where available
- Towers, orientations, unit mix, elevators/stairs
- Delivery standard and expected delivery date

## 5. Layouts

For each main layout:

- Room arrangement and area
- Floor, orientation, window/ventilation observation from the floor plan
- Living/dining form, balcony, bathroom layout, corridor waste
- Suitability for the user's family/budget
- Actual floor-plan image or link; mark unavailable if missing

## 6. Schools

Mandatory fields:

- Current official elementary school zone
- Current official junior-high school zone
- Source and effective year
- Distance/route
- Market tier, clearly labeled non-official
- Promotion/academic metric if a reliable public source exists; otherwise state “no comparable public metric”
- Annual re-zoning/no-guarantee warning

## 7. Metro and commute

- Nearest metro line and station
- Walking distance/time and route basis
- Major roads, bus, congestion, and commute to the user's known work areas
- Parking and car mobility if relevant

## 8. Adverse factors and surrounding uses

Checklist:

- Highways, railways, elevated roads, noise barriers
- High-voltage lines, substations, transformers
- Garbage transfer/processing, drainage, exhaust outlets
- Hospitals, funeral/cemetery-related uses, religious venues if noise/traffic-related
- Industrial/logistics pollution, contaminated or reclaimed land
- Commercial/nightlife crowding
- Future construction, vacant land, planning uncertainty
- Flood/waterlogging reports or low-lying terrain
- Internal不利因素公示 from the sales office

Label every item as confirmed / map-observed / sales-office-disclosed / unverified.

## 9. Comparables and liquidity

- 3–5 new/second-hand comparables in the same板块
- Compare price, age, metro, school, product, community quality
- Future resale/rental audience and likely liquidity

## 10. Final risk and verification list

- Developer/project risk
- Pre-sale and funds-supervision risk
- Price premium risk
- School re-zoning risk
- Delivery/quality risk
- Exact questions/documents the buyer must request before paying
